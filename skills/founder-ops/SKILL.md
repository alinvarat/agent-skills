# Founder operations

1. Read the brief and available `founder/idea.md`, `offer.md` and `numbers.json`. Map the daily fulfilment cycle from input to delivery, returns/support and reorder; mark customer-visible steps and capacity bottlenecks.
2. Research real suppliers for material inputs/equipment. Record price or quote route, currency, quantity, tax/shipping, minimum order, lead time, terms, URL and date. Unknown quotes stay unknown. Compare like-for-like costs; update a financial input only when that update is in scope, preserve its previous assumption, and rerun CFO calculations. Otherwise mark the update pending.
3. Define roles, hours and weekly rota for planned volume and a slow first month. Source or label wage assumptions; include applicable payroll costs rather than wages alone.
4. Write short numbered SOPs for opening, core fulfilment, complaints, closing and reorder. Select only tools needed for payment/store/scheduling/bookkeeping/inventory; cite current product costs when recommending them.
5. For the actual jurisdiction, check official sources for registration, permits, tax and insurance. Mark what needs authority/professional confirmation. Do not present a generic list as verified local compliance.
6. Add material risks with likelihood, impact, owner and fallback, proportional to the business. Write `founder/ops.md` with the cycle, suppliers, staffing, SOPs, tools, compliance and risks.

Done: capacity, staffing and costs agree; quotes/requirements have sources and dates; assumptions and pending updates are visible. A plan does not authorize purchases, hiring, registrations or supplier messages. Keep prices unless the user explicitly changes them.
