# Patterns to fix
## English
1. Significance inflation ("pivotal", "testament to", "evolving landscape"): state what happened.
2. Superficial -ing tails ("highlighting", "ensuring", "showcasing"): delete or give a real source.
3. Promotional words ("vibrant", "groundbreaking", "stunning"): neutral description.
4. Vague attribution ("experts believe"): name the source or delete.
5. AI vocabulary (additionally, delve, crucial, intricate, tapestry, underscore, foster): plain words.
6. Copula avoidance ("serves as", "boasts"): use "is" or "has".
7. "Not just X, it's Y": say the point directly.
8. Forced groups of three; synonym cycling; false "from X to Y" ranges.
9. Em dash overuse, mid-sentence colons, bold on every noun, inline-header bullet lists, title-case headings, decorative emoji, curly quotes.
10. Chatbot phrases ("I hope this helps", "Great question!"), cutoff disclaimers, generic upbeat conclusions.
11. Filler ("in order to" -> "to", "it is important to note that" -> delete); stacked hedging -> "may".

## Thai
1. Stock openers and bridges used in every paragraph: "ในยุคปัจจุบัน", "ในโลกที่...", "นอกจากนี้", "อย่างไรก็ตาม", "ทั้งนี้". Keep one only where it carries a real contrast.
2. "ไม่เพียงแต่...แต่ยัง..." and other parallel set phrases: say the point once.
3. Inflated praise words ("ล้ำสมัย", "เหนือชั้น", "ครบวงจร") without a fact behind them.
4. Machine-formal register where the author normally writes plainly; match the owner's concise everyday Thai.
5. Translated word order and heavy "การ/ความ" nominalization ("การทำการตรวจสอบ" -> "ตรวจ").
6. Restating the question or ending with a summary sentence that repeats the body.
These are heuristics; keep a phrase if it is the clearest way to say the thing.
